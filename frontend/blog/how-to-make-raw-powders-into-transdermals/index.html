How to make RAW POWDERS into TRANSDERMALS- 

By Wesley Inman-

First purchase IRON LEGION SALVO 

LINK TO BUY

http://stores.gymntonic.com/iron-legion-salvo-make...



Each Raw powder has a certain cut off point, whereas you can suspend it into the salvo..for some it is said to be 50mgs others 100mgs..I have gone above and beyond these numbers and succesfully suspended more, but never had labs to show the potency per mg..only personal experience to tell you that it did indeed dose higher. Plus most powders are white with some crystals..the Salvo is almost like an oil, and u can see if it dissolved fully..even if you get it to dissolve 90%..this is sufficient.

Iron Legion Salvo is a 30mL bottle at all times.

1)Boil a large pan of water...

2) Take the salvo and unscrew the lid, it has a dropper built in with measurements, 1/2 cc, etc......dump in raw powder to your desired dosage (for epi andro I used about 100-150mgs or 4.5 grams of raw powder. Slowly pour this into the Salvo (you will need a plastic funnel or homeade funnel since the opening is very very small, literally maybe 1/4 of an inch top)

3) Once powder is poured in..use something sterile to stir..I use a metal stirring tool..heated prior to use.

4) Put the lid back on the salvo with the powder now inside it and shake vigorously. Carefully remove the lid off the salvo which contains the dropper built in.

5) Take coffee cup or other container that you can fill with boiling water...Take some of the boiling water and pour into the coffee cup. Take the Salvo bottle and carefully place it into the heated water inside the cup..the water level should be just to the top or neck of the salvo..but no higher..do not get water inside the solution. Remove the rest of the water in the pan to continue boiling.

6) Let sit 5 minutes. Stir the solution again. Remove from the coffee cup and dump out existing hot water and pour in new boiling hot water to same desired level as previous.

7) Place the salvo bottle back into the new boiled water..let it sit for 5 minutes.

8) Repeat the above steps 4 times minimum, therefore allow the solution to be heated for at least 20+ minutes.

9)Remove the salvo solution the final time and leave out in a sterile environment for 10-20 minutes, or until room temperature. Feel free to cover with a large tupperware container or other large container if you are concerned that dust, or other particles, or bugs,etc..might fly into the solution.

10) Shake finished product vigorously. It is now ready for use.

**Be sure to shake very very well prior to each use.

11) Clean skin, or taking a hot shower will open the pores, allow skin to dry and then apply the solution you have made, aka your transdermal ProHormone. You can apply to any portion of the body.

I have found that the abdominals, foearms and biceps work best for me.but you can apply anywhere. Rotate application sites. (for example I use epi andro TD 1/2 cc on the left side of my stomach, and another 1/2 cc on the right side...2-3x per day)

Apply a few drops and rub in with one or two fingers..Avoid using an entire hand, you can use gloves as well, but be sure that the transdermal solution is absorbed prior to putting any clothes on that area.

**Be aware that just like test creams, etc..if you have children or gf/wives..the area that you applied the Transdermal (TD) PH to..can certainly have some absorption in those children/wives, etc..so proceed with caution.

Be very hesistant to allow the bare skin to touch another person where the TD solution was applied, unless you have showered and/or cleaned the area very thoroughly.

Enjoy!!!!!!!!!!
