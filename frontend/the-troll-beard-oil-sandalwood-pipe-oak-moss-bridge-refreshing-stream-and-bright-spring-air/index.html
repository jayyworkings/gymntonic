The Troll: Guardian of the Bridge

With the steadfast strength of stone and the ancient wisdom of the forest, The Troll stands as the protector of nature's crossings and the secrets of the wild. A master of resilience and fortitude, he carries the earthy essence of his domain, leaving an aura of timeless charm and rugged beauty. His presence is captured in a rich scent profile of Sandalwood Pipe, Oak Moss Bridge, Refreshing Stream, and Bright Spring Air—a fragrance as bold and enduring as the natural world itself.

Scent Profile:

  • Sandalwood Pipe: Warm and grounding, embodying The Troll's steady presence.
  • Oak Moss Bridge: Earthy and bold, reflecting the strength of his ancient domain.
  • Refreshing Stream: Crisp and invigorating, capturing the flow of life beneath the bridge.
  • Bright Spring Air: Uplifting and fresh, celebrating the vitality of nature.

The Troll isn’t just a fragrance—it’s an invitation to embrace the strength, beauty, and mystery of the wild. Crafted with premium ingredients, this beard oil nourishes and revitalizes your beard, surrounding you with an aroma that exudes confidence and timeless resilience.

Whether you’re walking through the forest or forging your own path, The Troll is your ultimate grooming companion. Let your beard journey reflect the spirit of Boron’s unyielding guardian of the bridge.

What’s Included:
Beard Oil (1 oz): Hydrate and condition your beard with The Troll’s bold and enduring blend, featuring notes of sandalwood pipe, oak moss bridge, refreshing stream, and bright spring air.
