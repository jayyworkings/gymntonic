What is Selank?

Selank, previously known as TP-7, is a small protein that is artificially produced in Russia. It is a member of the class of synthetic peptides designed for therapeutic use. It is commonly prescribed as an anti-anxiety drug due to its minimal side effects and a lower risk of addiction. By boosting the levels of neurotransmitters (brain chemicals) such as serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine, Selank ultimately improves cognition and reduces stress. Because of the unique spectrum of the pharmacological action of Selank and its higher safety profile, scientists believe that synthetic peptides like Selank have the potential to replace tranquilizers, antidepressants, and stimulants.

How Selank Works?

Selank works by binding to GABA receptors. This in turn increases the levels of serotonin in the brain. Serotonin helps regulate anxiety, happiness, and mood. Selank also stimulates the production of brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), which are proteins involved in the formation of new nerve cells or neurons (neurogenesis) and neuronal survival.

What are the Side Effects of Selank?

Selank’s most frequent side effects are:

  • Anxiety
  • Headaches
  • Nausea
  • Dizziness
  • Fatigue

There are also some less frequently reported side effects of Selank, which include:

  • Insomnia
  • Irritability
  • Muscle cramps
  • Joint pain

Selank should be stopped if any of these side effects persist, or worsen.

Selank Withdrawals

Selank has a similar mechanism of action to some traditional anxiety medications. This leads many people to assume that Selank use is followed by withdrawals or a “comedown”. However, clinical studies show no serious side effects from Selank use, including withdrawals. Simply put, Selank has no sedating or negative cognitive side effects and has no associated addiction or withdrawal problems.
