Forbidden Ingredients

 

The rare botanicals included in the Vintage formula possibly added to the aromatherapeutic effect. The botanical component was created in 2005 under the tutelage of UK’s Chrissie Wildwood. She is a giant in the holistic aromatherapy world, author of Erotic Aromatherapy (and several others), and one of our original formulation team’s esteemed teachers. With her guidance, the original formulators developed a novel botanical extract of warming and sensual herbs that aged over several years, mellowing out over time. If it was even compliant today it would take 20 years to mature. 

 

Another forbidden set of ingredients that makes Vintage what it is are the additions of wild Himalayan Kasthuri deer musks from Tibet and Nepal. Cervid (deer, elk) pheromones are some of the most potent and long distance pheromones in the animal kingdom, just ask any hunter during open season – a trained nose can smell a trophy buck from 10-30 yards away! 

 

Unlike the North American deer, the Moschus Moschiferus musk deer produces highly revered medicinal musk in a gland near its navel called the musk pod. North American deer don’t even have this gland. The musk pod itself is a pheromone producing gland of the deer, used in nature for marking and mating. 

 

At one point they were thought to be “no-kill” musks, but that just simply wasn’t possible. Processing this pod unfortunately contributes to the demise of the deer, but it’s a potent men’s hormone medicine used in Ayurvedic practices and aromatherapy since time immemorial. 

 

Many of the compounds that make up the pod itself are possible pheromones that can’t be isolated or quantified. 

 

This was the reason why it was included in the original formula.

 

A complex bouquet of mammalian pheromones sourced from nature. 

 

…But at the cost of blood on our hands. 

 

So… that’s not happening again. 

 

In modern times the deer are farmed and harvested for the medicinal values of the pods, but farmed Kasthuri deer just don’t have the oomph as the wild deer did. Those components aren’t even really available without overstepping some gravely unethical poaching realities, but because the formula was created in 2005, it’s grandfathered as a collector’s piece.

 

All these complex notes are nestled between the Akuto notes and bloom when applied. The deer musk is the deep base note that will linger on for days.
