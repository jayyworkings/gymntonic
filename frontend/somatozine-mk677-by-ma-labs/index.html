Even worse, certain aspects of this process are outside of their control entirely, leaving the consumer at the mercy of chance. Now, I am not attempting to disparage these home-based companies—not in the least— as I believe the majority do care about putting out a quality product (a truth reflected at least partially in the results and feedback of their customers). However, there is a distinct advantage to having these products made within GMP facilities.
